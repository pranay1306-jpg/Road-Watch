The Challenge of Road Safety & Transparency
	Hazardous Conditions: Poor road quality contributes to accidents.
	Reporting Friction: Citizens lack a simple platform to report issues.
	Opaque Maintenance: Limited transparency on public spending.
	Delayed Action: Issues remain unresolved for months.
Introducing RoadWatch
	Citizen Reporting: Simple web interface to log road defects.
	Live Tracking: Dashboard to view issues and track resolution status.
	Spending Transparency: Bridge between municipal budgets and ground repairs.
	Accessible to All: No complex logins required for reporting.
Technical Architecture
	Frontend Layer: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS (No heavy frameworks).
	Backend API Layer: Python & FastAPI for high performance.
	Database Layer: SQLite with SQLAlchemy ORM.
	Architecture Flow: Client Browser <-> REST APIs <-> DB Session.
How RoadWatch Works (Demo Flow)
	Step 1: Land on home page and learn about the mission.
	Step 2: Submit details of a hazard via the Report form.
	Step 3: Browse the Issues list with live filtering.
	Step 4: Review Analytics and ward-level budget expenditures.
Future Vision & Impact
	Immediate Impact: Improves civic engagement and saves lives.
	AI Prioritization: Machine Learning to categorize severity.
	Computer Vision: Auto-verify pothole dimensions from photos.
	Open Gov Integration: Connect with real municipal treasury APIs.
